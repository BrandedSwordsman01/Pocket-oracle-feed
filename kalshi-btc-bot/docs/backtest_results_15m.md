# KXBTC15M strategy study

Generated by `research/run_15m_study.py` in 36s. Data: 5686 settled 15-minute windows, 79604 minute-level decision rows (14 to 1 min before close), 2026-09-23 .. 2026-11-22. Bitstamp: yes; Deribit DVOL: yes.

- **Train** 2026-09-23 .. 2026-10-22 (2782 windows) · **Validation** 2026-10-23 .. 2026-11-06 (1424) · **Test** 2026-11-07 .. 2026-11-22 (1480), touched once.
- Execution: buy at the ask / sell at the bid, Kalshi taker fee on every order, 10 contracts per trade, stale quotes (>180s) never traded, max spread 5¢ unless stated.
- Per-contract P&L is in cents after fees. t = mean / standard error. A result counts as real only if it holds on validation **and** test with t > 2.

## 1. Settlement benchmark vs spot (is the 'lag' real?)

Each window's strike is the BRTI 60-second average at the open. Compared with the Coinbase close of the same minute: mean basis **+0.39 bp**, std **2.93 bp**, 95% of windows within ±5.6 bp (n=5686).
Bitstamp vs Coinbase at decision times: mean +0.17 bp, std 1.14 bp.
For scale: median 1-minute BTC volatility is 4.4 bp, so a 2.9 bp basis error is about 0.7 minutes of noise. Strategy B is tested both with and without the basis correction.

## 9. Conclusions

| Strategy | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A RSI momentum | **Rejected** | 4/96 variants positive on train, 1/96 on train+validation; selected config −0.71¢ on test. The "100/100 profitable" claim is not reproduced with executable prices and fees. |
| B Momentum / lag | **Not validated** (weak candidate for forward paper testing only) | Selected config positive on train, validation and test (+1.3/+2.2/+2.8¢) but t < 1; only 4/28 configs positive over 60 days. |
| C Binary fair value | **Rejected as a stand-alone trading signal** | 0/216 configs positive on train. The model is over-confident at the extremes (predicts 0.85 → realised 0.78; 0.035 → 0.062), and the market's Brier beats it (0.1855 vs 0.1965 test). Deribit DVOL made it worse than realized vol, consistent with the published finding that Kalshi's variance risk premium is only weakly linked to DVOL. |
| D Flip/barrier filter | **No value as a filter or exit** | 0/9 positive; flip exits cut winners as often as losers. Useful as a risk display only. |
| E Ensemble | **Overfit** | Train +4.19¢ → validation −1.23¢ → test −4.24¢; the market mid carries almost all the weight (+1.12); other features add < 0.0002 log loss. |
| Blend (app v0.5) | **Earlier +5.3¢ result not confirmed** | Walk-forward over 60 days: +1.15¢/contract, n=370, t=0.51. The 14-day result was within noise. |
| Leading side | **Rejected** | −2.2¢ on test, consistent with the public vendor study. |

**Bottom line:** across about 400 configurations and 5,686 windows, nothing shows positive expectancy after costs that survives validation and test with t > 2.
Kalshi's 15-minute prices are well calibrated (market Brier 0.1855 on test), and the gap between any minute-resolution public-data model and the
market is smaller than the round-trip cost of trading it.
